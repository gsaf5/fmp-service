# Restricted Entities — Trading Prohibited

## Critical Compliance Rule

Gary is PROHIBITED from trading any security on this list due to Jen's professional
relationships through her employer. These are not preferences — they are compliance
requirements. Violations could have serious professional and legal consequences for Jen.

**When a match is found:**
1. IMMEDIATELY flag the security as RESTRICTED — DO NOT RECOMMEND
2. State clearly: "⛔ RESTRICTED — Jen's company has a relationship with [Entity]. You cannot trade this security."
3. Do not provide buy/sell analysis on restricted securities
4. Suggest an alternative if relevant (e.g., a competing company or sector ETF)

**How to check:**
- Check both the entity name AND the ticker symbol
- Check partial matches — if "Affirm" appears in a recommendation, check if AFRM is on the list
- For ETFs, check if any top holdings are restricted entities
- When in doubt, flag it and let Gary decide
